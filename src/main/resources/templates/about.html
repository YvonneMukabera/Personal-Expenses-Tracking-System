What the system does

The application helps a user build a reliable picture of personal cash flow. Expenses are recorded with category, quantity, unit cost, total, and date. Income is stored by month, then compared against spending on the dashboard.

The focus is practical: quick entry, clean transaction lists, useful filters, monthly and yearly summaries, and simple exports for reporting.

Data Handling

  • Expense records are connected to the signed-in user.
  • Dashboard calculations use saved expenses and monthly income.
  • Pagination keeps the list readable as records grow.
  • Year and month filters help users review specific periods.
  • CSV export supports offline review and simple reporting.
